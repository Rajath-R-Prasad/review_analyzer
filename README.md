# 🛒 Product Review Sentiment Analyzer

Imagine a product with hundreds of customer reviews. Some customers love it, some are disappointed, while others have mixed opinions. Reading every review manually to understand what customers generally feel about the product can take a lot of time. In this project, we’ll build a **Product Review Sentiment Analyzer** that takes multiple customer reviews and analyzes the words used in each review to determine whether it is **🟢 POSITIVE, 🔴 NEGATIVE, or 🟡 NEUTRAL**.

But we won’t stop at analyzing individual reviews. The program will bring all the results together, **count and compare the sentiments**, calculate their percentages, and determine the **overall sentiment of the product**. Finally, it will generate a simple report that answers the bigger question: **“What do customers generally think about this product?”**
